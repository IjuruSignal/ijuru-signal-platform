# Delivery and pitch: Ijuru Signal

## MVP delivery

1. Confirm competition eligibility and deadlines; interview target users and select a narrow pilot with local practitioners.
2. Implement one complete workflow described in [the product brief](02-product.md), including consent, source attribution, error states and a human verification route. Label fixture data explicitly.
3. Obtain data access and permissions, validate the workflow with experts and representative users, then record results, operating costs and unresolved risks before expansion.

## Judge presentation (maximum 10 slides)

1. A specific user and problem; distinguish an illustrative scenario from real testimony.
2. Evidence and existing alternatives, citing the research.
3. The proposed workflow and the gap it aims to address.
4. A truthful demo, including missing data and failure states.
5. The technical mechanism and evidence sources.
6. Safety, uncertainty and human oversight.
7. Accessibility and inclusion.
8. MVP milestones, feasibility and costs.
9. Pilot evaluation and a sustainable business hypothesis.
10. Team, concrete ask and next milestone.

For the three-minute pitch, allocate roughly 25 seconds to the problem, 35 to context, 60 to the demonstration, 35 to the mechanism and limitations, and 25 to the team and ask. Verify official stage requirements.
